# Sachin Systems Inc. (SS) — Business Model & Operations Brief

## Executive Summary
Sachin Systems Inc. operates as a specialized **R2/ISO-certified secondary mobile asset processor, circular economy distributor, and cross-border arbitrage trader**, bridging the secondary smartphone supply chain between North America, the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia.

---

## 1. Core Market Niche & Operational Capabilities

### A. Secondary Mobile Device Lifecycle & ITAD
* **Asset Types**: Certified pre-owned, carrier returns, trade-ins, off-lease corporate stock, and enterprise liquidations.
* **Upstream Standards**: Sources tested and functional mobile inventory exclusively from R2-certified vendors.
* **Downstream Environmental Compliance**: Focus materials and electronic scrap resulting from manual dismantling are directed to R2-certified smelters and recyclers with a strict **Zero-Landfill** policy.

### B. Tier-1 Industry Certifications (The Strategic Moat)
* **R2 (Responsible Recycling)**: Crucial credential enabling access to tier-1 telecom carriers and box-retail enterprise liquidation contracts prohibited from uncertified brokers.
* **ISO 14001**: Environmental Management Systems certification.
* **ISO 45001**: Occupational Health & Safety certification.
* **EHSMS**: Environmental, Health & Safety Management System.

### C. Testing, Diagnostic Automation & Data Security
* **Diagnostic Software**: Enterprise automation via **PhoneCheck** for battery health, screen originality, component functionality, and carrier lock status.
* **Data Sanitization**: Certified data destruction adhering to NIST 800-88 guidelines with complete audit logs.
* **Clean-ESN / Anti-Theft Verification**: Verified against blacklist and theft databases; certified legal for resale.

## 2. Global Dual-Hub Infrastructure

### 1. United States Headquarters & Operations Hub
* **Location**: `8029 Almeda Rd, Houston, TX 77054 USA`
* **Function**: Domestic device intake, serialization, PhoneCheck testing, data wiping, and North American distribution.

### 2. Middle East International Re-Export Hub
* **Location**: `Warehouse #D20, Dubai Airport Freezone (DAFZA), Dubai, UAE`
* **Function**: Tax-free international distribution and air-cargo re-export serving wholesale buyers across EMEA and South Asia with zero local import duty friction.

---

## 3. Commercial, Fulfillment & Risk Management Policies
* **100% Upfront Pre-Payment**: Elimination of B2B accounts receivable credit risk.
* **FOB Warehouse Logistics**: Buyer-assumed freight and transit liability; optional client-purchased transit insurance.
* **Photo-Verified Packaging**: Pre-dispatch photo documentation of packed boxes sent to buyers alongside tracking details.
* **Grading & Returns**: Strict wholesale "as-is" grading standard with a strict **7-day RMA window**.
* **Primary Sales Channel**: Dedicated sales representatives leveraging **direct WhatsApp broadcast channels** and inventory lists.
