Chapter 08 • Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, refurbishment, and B2B terms.

All devices are procured directly through authorized carrier and vendor auctions from Tier-1 US operators (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile). We do not purchase unvetted grey-market stock. Every unit is screened to ensure 100% clean ESN and legal resale status.
Our technicians perform deep hardware restoration: cleanroom display re-lamination, logic board micro-soldering (resolving audio IC, charging IC, and baseband issues), FaceID optical realignment, battery module renewal, and camera sensor stabilization.
For initial sample and verification orders, our MOQ starts at 25 units. For ongoing commercial wholesale allocations, standard tiers run from 100 to 2,500+ units per shipment.
We stand behind our PhoneCheck certified grading with a strict 7-day inspection window from physical delivery. If any device fails functional verification, our team issues an authorized RMA number for immediate replacement or invoice credit.
Orders are dispatched under standard international wholesale terms: 100% upfront bank wire transfer before warehouse release. All shipments are FOB Warehouse (Houston, TX or DAFZA Dubai, UAE) with pre-shipment photo verification.
